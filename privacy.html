AikonAu Shop Research privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

AikonAu Shop Research is a private tool used only by the owner of the AikonAu Etsy shop. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

It reads Pinterest trending search keywords, through the official Pinterest API, to research which design templates people are looking for. It does not read, create or change any Pins or boards.

What data it handles

It stores only the trending keywords Pinterest returns and the login token for the shop owner's own Pinterest account. It collects no personal information about anyone else.

Storage and sharing

Login tokens are stored encrypted in a private database. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Revoking the app in Pinterest settings ends its access at any time.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the AikonAu Etsy shop.
